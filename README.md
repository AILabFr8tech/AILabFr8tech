### 🧠 About Us
The AI Cognitive Lab of Freight Technologies (Fr8Tech) was founded in 2025 to rethink **cross-border freight between the United States and Mexico**.
We build AI systems that go beyond prediction and optimization: systems that understand the details of real freight
movements, grounded in data from thousands of shipments, lanes and cross-border trade patterns.

We work at the intersection of **research and industry**: our models ship in commercial products, and our
research is published openly.

---

### 🎯 Mission
- Develop AI-powered logistics products with tangible, real-world applications
- Leverage cognitive architectures to enhance decision-making and automation
- Foster collaboration between academia and industry to advance AI research
- Attract and nurture top AI talent to build a networked team of leading technologists

---

### 🚀 What We Do
- 📄 **Document Intelligence**: reading freight paperwork the way people do. Multi-model signature and stamp detection, bilingual (English/Spanish) OCR and spatial document understanding automate checks such as proof-of-delivery validation.
- 🤖 **Agentic AI**: AI agents that act, not just predict. Our agent platform **Zayren** automates the search for and procurement of U.S.–Mexico cross-border and domestic Mexican trucking capacity, and our negotiation agents bargain over freight rates.
- 📈 **Pricing Analytics & Prediction**: freight-rate prediction and pricing models for cross-border and domestic lanes, trained on real shipment data.
- 🕸️ **Network Intelligence**: graph-based algorithms, rather than static database lookups, rank carriers by their real-world performance and history on each corridor.
- 🏗️ **AI Transformation**: the Fr8Tech AI Transformation Framework (FATF), a governed ecosystem that embeds agentic AI across software development and operational workflows.

### 🔬 Open Research
**[We Have a Deal](https://github.com/AILabFr8tech/wehaveadeal)**: an open arena for automated freight-rate negotiation. Plug in your own shipper or carrier agent
(rules, search, machine learning, reinforcement learning or LLMs) and benchmark it against the nine reference
architectures from our paper *Rules, Search, and Learning Under Scarcity: A Controlled Tournament of Nine Bilateral
Negotiation Architectures*. Everything runs on your own machine, and every result in the paper is reproducible.

**Research areas**
- Multi-agent negotiation and mechanism design
- Reinforcement learning and imitation learning for pricing
- LLM agents for logistics decision-making
- Document understanding with vision and language models
- Graph learning on freight networks
